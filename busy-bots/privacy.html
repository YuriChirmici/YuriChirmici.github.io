Privacy Policy

Busy Bots: Cube Art Puzzle

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short

Busy Bots is an offline single-player puzzle game. It does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other contact details. Your progress is stored on your device and, if you use Google Play Games, backed up to your Google account.

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show and measure those ads, as described below. Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Play statistics are sent to Unity Analytics only if you say yes when the game asks you, and you can change your mind at any time.

You do not have to give any personal data to play. Every choice described here (ad consent, analytics, Play Games) can be refused, and the game still works.

Who is responsible

The controller for the personal data described in this policy is the developer of Busy Bots, YukiForge Games, an independent game developer based in Bulgaria (European Union). Contact for all privacy questions and requests: yukiforge.games@gmail.com

The developer runs no servers of its own and does not receive data from the game directly. The data described below is processed by Google and Unity, as service providers for the game, and in part as controllers under their own privacy policies.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels won, a level you left halfway, coins, boosters, the pictures in your collection, robot skins, purchases, play time, and your sound, music, language and privacy settings) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to the developer. It is normally removed when you uninstall the game, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the game signs in to it and keeps a copy of your progress in Play Games Saved Games, so it comes back on a new phone or after reinstalling. The copy is stored by Google in your Google account, not on the developer's servers, and the developer cannot read it. For this Google processes your Play Games player ID and gamer name. You can play without Play Games; then progress stays on the device only. You can delete the saved data from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Legal basis: providing the cloud save you chose to use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Play statistics (Unity Analytics), only if you say yes

After level 5 the game asks every player, in every country, whether it may share play statistics. Nothing in this section is collected before you answer, and if you say no, nothing is ever sent. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

If you say yes, the game sends play statistics to Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services, Unity Technologies), so the developer can see which levels are too hard, where players stop and how boosters, ads and purchases are used. What is sent:

  • gameplay events: the tutorial, a level started, won, lost or left (with the level number, time spent, robots sent and boosters used), boosters used or bought, continues, coins earned and spent, ads offered and watched, the shop opened, purchases, and the robot skins chosen;
  • a random install ID and user ID generated by Unity (not your name or account);
  • device and operating-system information (model, OS version, language);
  • an approximate country, derived by Unity from your IP address.

The purpose is to improve the levels and the game's balance. In the game's settings you can:

  • switch off Share play statistics: your consent is withdrawn and collection stops at once;
  • tap Delete my play stats: the game stops collecting and asks Unity to delete the play statistics already collected from this installation.

Unity keeps the data according to its own retention rules, until you ask for deletion. Unity's privacy policy applies: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived;
  • device and operating-system information;
  • how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown; where US state privacy laws apply, it offers the opt-out those laws require. Without consent you see non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy options in the game's settings.

Legal basis: your consent for personalised ads and for storing or reading information on your device where consent is required (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention, the legitimate interest in funding a free game with ads (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Some ads are optional: you choose to watch one for an extra garage after a lost level or for double coins after a win. Other ads are shown only after a finished level, never during play, never in the first levels, and at most once every few minutes of play. Buying "Remove ads" removes the ads shown between levels; the optional reward ads stay available.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases (such as Remove ads) are processed by Google Play. The game receives only a confirmation that a purchase was made and which product it was. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Google and are never seen by the game or the developer. What you bought is also recorded in your local save, and Google Play remains the record of what you own, so your purchases come back on a new device.

Legal basis: performing the purchase you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

If you write to us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. We keep the correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request.

Legal basis: answering your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).

Who receives data

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC): AdMob ads and the consent form (User Messaging Platform), Google Play Games saved games, Google Play Billing.
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services): play statistics, only with your consent.

These companies may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under the safeguards they provide, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. The developer does not sell your personal data and shares it with no one else.

How long data is kept

  • Progress on your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Cloud save: in your Google account until you delete it there.
  • Play statistics: according to Unity's retention rules, or until you tap "Delete my play stats".
  • Advertising data: according to Google's retention rules.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request.

Children

Busy Bots is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device settings.
  • Change or withdraw your ad consent in the game's settings ("Privacy options").
  • Say no to analytics, switch it off later ("Share play statistics"), or delete the statistics already sent ("Delete my play stats") in the game's settings.
  • Uninstall the game to delete the progress stored on your device.

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before. The developer keeps no personal data about you on its own servers; for data processed by Google or Unity, use the settings above or their privacy policies, or contact us and we will help.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in the EU country where you live or work, or in Bulgaria with the Commission for Personal Data Protection: cpdp.bg
