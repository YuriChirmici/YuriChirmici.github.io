Privacy Policy

Raylume: Mirror Laser Puzzle

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short

Raylume is an offline single-player puzzle game. It does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other contact details. Your progress is stored on your device and, if you use Google Play Games, backed up to your Google account.

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show and measure those ads, as described below. Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. If you allow analytics, the game also sends anonymous play statistics through Unity Analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (solved levels, the stars lit on your sky map, hints, purchases, the sky theme and glass set you chose, sound, music, language and other settings) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to the developer. It is normally removed when you uninstall the game, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the game signs in to it and keeps a copy of your progress in Play Games Saved Games, so it comes back on a new phone or after reinstalling. The copy is stored by Google in your Google account, not on the developer's servers, and the developer cannot read it. For this Google processes your Play Games player ID and gamer name. You can play without Play Games; then progress stays on the device only. You can delete the saved data from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Play statistics (Unity Analytics), if you allow analytics

This section applies only if you allow analytics. The game asks before it sends anything, the legal basis is your consent, and if you say no, nothing in this section is collected.

To see which levels are too hard, where players stop and how hints, ads and purchases are used, the game then sends play statistics to Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies). What is sent:

  • gameplay events: the tutorial, a level started, solved or left (with time spent and hints used), hints used, ads watched, the shop opened, purchases started, completed or failed, and the styles chosen;
  • a random install ID and user ID generated by Unity (not your name or account);
  • device and operating-system information (model, OS version, language);
  • an approximate country, derived by Unity from your IP address.

The purpose is to improve the levels and the game's balance. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the game's settings; collection stops at once. The data is kept according to Unity's default retention. Unity's privacy policy applies: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived;
  • device and operating-system information;
  • how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. Without consent you see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy options in the game's settings.

Some ads are optional: you choose to watch them for a free hint. Other ads are shown between solved levels, never during a puzzle. Buying "Remove ads" removes the ads shown between levels.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases

Purchases (hint packs, Remove ads, sky themes and glass sets) are processed by Google Play. The game receives only a confirmation that a purchase was made and which product it was. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Google and are never seen by the game or the developer. What you bought is also recorded in your local save, and Google Play remains the record of what you own, so Remove ads and the styles you bought come back on their own on a new device.

Children

Raylume is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device settings.
  • Change or withdraw your ad consent in the game's settings ("Privacy options").
  • Say no to analytics, or switch it off later in the game's settings.
  • Uninstall the game to delete the progress stored on your device.

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Bulgaria, the Commission for Personal Data Protection: cpdp.bg). The developer keeps no personal data about you on its own servers; for data processed by Google or Unity, see their privacy policies above, or contact us and we will help.
